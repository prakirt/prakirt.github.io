Prakirt Raj Jhunjhunwala

Postdoctoral Scientist, Amazon FBA Science

I am a Postdoctoral Scientist at Amazon within the FBA Science team, where I develop optimization algorithms and scalable methodologies for large-scale supply chain, logistics, and inventory management. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Scholar at Columbia Business School, where I collaborated with Jing Dong and Yash Kanoria.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and a Master's in Mathematics from the Georgia Institute of Technology. My doctoral research was advised by Siva Theja Maguluri and received an Honorable Mention for the ACM SIGMETRICS 2023 Doctoral Dissertation Award. Prior to my time at Georgia Tech, I completed my B. Tech. in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, working under the guidance of Sharayu Moharir.

My research interests span operations research, marketplace design, and applied probability, with a particular specialization in design and performance analysis of complex queueing systems and service platforms. Outside of research, I enjoy hiking, playing badminton, and cooking.
