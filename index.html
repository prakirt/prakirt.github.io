Prakirt Raj Jhunjhunwala

Applied Scientist, Amazon.com Inc · Bellevue, Washington

I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon.com Inc in Bellevue, Washington. At Amazon, I develop optimization methods and scalable decision-making tools for large-scale supply chain, logistics, and inventory management problems. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Columbia Business School (2023–2025), where I worked with Jing Dong and Yash Kanoria.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and a Master's in Mathematics from the Georgia Institute of Technology. My doctoral research was advised by Siva Theja Maguluri and received an Honorable Mention for the ACM SIGMETRICS 2023 Doctoral Dissertation Award. Prior to my time at Georgia Tech, I completed my B. Tech. in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, working under the guidance of Sharayu Moharir.

My research interests span operations research, marketplace design, and applied probability, including queueing systems, stochastic processing networks, and service platforms. Outside of research, I enjoy hiking, playing badminton, and cooking.
